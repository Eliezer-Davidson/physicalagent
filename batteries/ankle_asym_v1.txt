# ankle_asym_v1 -- designed probe for the UNEXPLAINED right/left ankle
# thermal asymmetry (right_ankle_roll 42->71 C vs left 40->44 C over 7 min,
# MEASURED 2026-08-19; peak rise 137.7 C/min during walking).
#
# Structure: alternating walk blocks and long stands, then yaw both ways.
# The stands are the experiment as much as the walks -- they separate "heats
# under load" from "heats under balance", and carry the cooling constants.
# The thermal watch is armed during any --script run; warn is 80 C.
#
# While this runs, ALSO check the physical hypotheses the data cannot see:
# harness pulling one leg, foot hanging at a different angle, cable tension.
# The ankle is a coupled pitch/roll linkage in series mode -- compare BOTH
# ankle motors on BOTH sides, in temp_driver, never temp_winding.

mark battery-start
status
mark stand-cold
sleep 60

mark walk-block-1
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
mark stand-recover-1
sleep 45

mark walk-block-2
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
mark stand-recover-2
sleep 45

mark walk-block-3
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
walk 0.2 5
sleep 1
mark stand-recover-3
sleep 45

mark turn-block
turn 0.3 4
sleep 1
turn -0.3 4
sleep 1
turn 0.3 4
sleep 1
turn -0.3 4
sleep 1

mark stand-final
sleep 45
stop
status
mark battery-end
